Features

Everything you need to clip

From AI-powered selection to platform-ready export — a complete pipeline.

AI Segment Selection

Pydantic AI analyzes transcripts to pick 3–7 viral moments with detailed virality scoring.

Face-Aware Cropping

MediaPipe → OpenCV DNN → Haar cascade fallback chain keeps faces perfectly centered.

Word-Synced Captions

WhisperX word timestamps rendered as styled, animated captions with multiple templates.

Virality Scoring

Every clip scored on hook, engagement, value & shareability — know what works before exporting.

Caption Templates

Customizable animated templates with multiple styles to match your brand identity.

Transition Effects

Optional intro/outro effects between clips for a polished, professional finish.

B-Roll Overlays

Pexels integration for stock footage overlays that enhance visual appeal and retention.

YouTube Upload

Multi-channel OAuth upload directly from the app to your YouTube channels.

Real-Time Progress

SSE stream from worker to frontend — see transcription, analysis, and rendering live.
